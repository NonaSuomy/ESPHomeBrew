NetSurf on the ESP32-P4

This is the NetSurf web browser's framebuffer frontend, running as a PAPP. Type an address in the bar at the top: http:// and https:// sites, files on the card and NetSurf's own pages work (no JavaScript). A name typed without http:// or https:// opens with HTTPS. Text is drawn with FreeType in the DejaVu fonts; pages can show PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, ICO, WebP and SVG images.

HTTPS connections are made and checked by the loader against the firmware's certificate bundle. A site it cannot verify does not open, and there is no way to skip the check.

Try

  • about:hotlist: your bookmarks
  • about:history: the pages you have visited
  • about:about: NetSurf's internal pages
  • about:config: its options
  • about:credits and about:licence
  • DuckDuckGo Lite: web search without JavaScript
  • Wikipedia (mobile pages)
  • text.npr.org: news in plain HTML
  • info.cern.ch: the first web site
  • FrogFind: a search engine for old browsers
  • 68k.news: news in plain HTML
  • file:///sd/roms/netsurf/index.html: your own start page on the card

Controls

  • Touch: tap to click, drag the page to scroll, drag the scroll bars.
  • Tapping a text field opens the on-screen keyboard; it goes away when the field loses the caret. The keyboard button in the bottom right corner opens it by hand. After a USB keyboard has been used it no longer opens by itself.
  • The star in the toolbar bookmarks the page (filled: bookmarked; tap again to remove the bookmark). The button next to it opens the bookmarks.
  • USB mouse: point and click; hold the middle button and move to scroll.
  • USB keyboard: type into the address bar or page; arrows, Page Up/Down, Home/End scroll. F6 or Ctrl-L: new address. F5: reload. Alt+Left/Right: back/forward. Ctrl-+/-/0: zoom. Ctrl-D: bookmark the page. Ctrl-B: bookmarks. Ctrl-H: history.
  • Gamepad: A click, B back, Y reload, Select address bar, Start Enter, L/R page up/down.
  • Menu or Escape held for 3 seconds, or the grey close button at the top right, quits to the launcher.

Files

NetSurf keeps its files in /sd/roms/netsurf/: Choices (options, in NetSurf's key:value format), Cookies, Hotlist (bookmarks), URLs (history), and optionally Messages or its own copies of default.css, welcome.html and the other built-in resources, which then replace the built-in ones. Without that folder, cookies, bookmarks and history last until NetSurf quits. Create an empty file named verbose there for NetSurf's log in the device log.

Fonts

The built-in fonts are DejaVu Sans, Serif and Sans Mono, regular and bold, cut down to Latin, Greek, Cyrillic and common symbols; italics are drawn slanted. Other characters show as boxes. For all of DejaVu's characters and real italics, copy the DejaVu font files (DejaVuSans.ttf, DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf, DejaVuSans-Oblique.ttf, DejaVuSans-BoldOblique.ttf, and the same for DejaVuSerif (-Italic, -BoldItalic) and DejaVuSansMono) into /sd/roms/netsurf/fonts/; each file found there replaces its built-in font. The fb_face_* options in Choices can name other TrueType fonts. Web fonts (@font-face) are not loaded.
